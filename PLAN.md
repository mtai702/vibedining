# Vibe Dining redesign

The visitor delegates their own meal to AI, as they might delegate writing code. The work asks which experiences we want to hand over.

- Human restaurant menu: familiar dishes, descriptions and dollar prices.
- Two thirds menu, one third agent on desktop; stacked on mobile.
- Keep the opening question, locally hallucinated remark, and 100% artificial stamp.
- Prompts ask the agent to eat for the visitor. Scripted thoughts, responses and receipts report completion on their behalf.
- Preserve selection, streaming, cancellation, reset, keyboard controls and reduced motion.
- No real LLM, payment, food consumption, or persistent data.

## Trattoria refinement

- Replace the menu headline with a traditional restaurant masthead and six Italian course sections.
- Use token prices throughout. Slow the agent to course-by-course tasting with pauses between bites.
- Drop an itemized paper guest check over the menu only after a completed meal, with suggested token tips.
- Follow up with a fictional offer to enter the guest's card. No payment UI or card storage.
- Verify receipt arithmetic, completion, cancellation, reset, encoding and local serving.

## Final interaction refinements

- Selectable percentage gratuities and editable custom token tips with live totals.
- Independent menu scrolling with aligned desktop panel heights and a stationary check overlay.
- Extended original tasting narratives for every dish.
- Three selectable fictional agents with distinct voices, captured per meal.

## Restaurant expansion

- Rename Al Dente-4o to Dentex throughout the UI and agent identity.
- Add five fictional restaurants with ten original dishes each, sectioned menus, long tasting scripts, and distinct visual accents.
- Lock restaurant choice during meals; clear pending orders and checks on restaurant switches.
- Capture restaurant identity in each conversation and bill, preserving token tips and existing chat history.
- Serve the restaurant catalog locally and include it in static deployment files.

## Fortune and live status

- Keep the spinner after the latest output with a sticky bottom position during streaming.
- Drop a fortune cookie with completed Chinese meal checks; reveal its note only on click or keyboard activation.
- Move the automation question from the page footer into that private reveal.
- Clear the cookie on restaurant switches, reset, and replacement checks.
